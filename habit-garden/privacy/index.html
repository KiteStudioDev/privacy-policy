PRIVACY POLICY

Your habits stay on your device.

Habits, timer state, streaks, rewards, collection choices, onboarding, and settings are stored in Chrome local extension storage. Habit Garden does not read webpage content, tabs, history, or website forms and has no advertising or analytics.

Optional purchases

Checkout is hosted by Lemon Squeezy. To restore Premium content, the extension sends the user-entered License Key, checkout email, and random device instance ID to Lemon Squeezy for activation, validation, refresh, or deactivation. Payment-card information is never handled by the extension.
