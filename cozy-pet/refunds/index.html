REFUND POLICY

Fair help when something does not work.

How to request help or a refund for the digital Complete Cozy Pack.

Digital purchase

The Complete Cozy Pack is a digital, one-time purchase delivered through a Gumroad License Key. It is not a subscription.

Technical problems

If the pack cannot be activated because of a verified technical problem that KiteStudio cannot resolve, contact kitestudio.dev@gmail.com within 14 days of purchase. Include the Gumroad order number and a description of the issue. Do not include payment-card details or publish the License Key.

Eligibility and processing

Refund eligibility may be limited after digital content has been activated, except where applicable consumer law requires otherwise. Approved refunds are processed through Gumroad to the original payment method and remain subject to Gumroad's mandatory fraud, chargeback, tax, and consumer-protection procedures.

Access after a refund

When Gumroad reports a purchase as refunded, disputed, or charged back, Cozy Pet removes premium entitlements after the next successful License Key verification. Previously cached access may remain temporarily until that verification occurs.

Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot be waived under applicable law.
