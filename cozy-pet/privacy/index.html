PRIVACY POLICY

Your little steps stay yours.

How KiteStudio Cozy Pet handles local progress, to-dos, and Gumroad License Key verification.

Data stored on your device

Cozy Pet stores pet progress, bond rewards, pending and completed to-dos, pet and room selections, active browsing-time totals, the Gumroad License Key entered by the user, a random installation identifier, product mapping, license status, and validation timestamps in Chrome's local extension storage.

Chrome's idle status is used only to pause XP while the browser is inactive. Cozy Pet does not inspect which websites you visit or read webpage content.

Data sent to Gumroad

When paid content is activated or refreshed, Cozy Pet sends the License Key, the public Gumroad product ID, and a flag that prevents verification from increasing the key's use count to Gumroad's License API over HTTPS. This is necessary to verify access to the Complete Cozy Pack.

Gumroad's response may temporarily include customer or order metadata. Cozy Pet uses only the license result, product mapping, refund or dispute status, and validation time. Customer, contact, payment, and order fields are not saved, displayed, analyzed, or made available to humans at KiteStudio through the extension.

Limited use

Information handled by Cozy Pet is used only for its companion, local productivity, and paid-content licensing features. It is not sold or used for advertising, creditworthiness, unrelated profiling, or other unrelated purposes.

Data we do not collect

Cozy Pet does not read or transmit webpage content, browsing history, URLs, passwords, website form entries, personal communications, location, or the text of your to-dos to KiteStudio. Payment-card and billing information is handled on Gumroad's hosted checkout and is not received by the extension.

Data removal

You can remove local extension data by uninstalling Cozy Pet or clearing its stored data in Chrome. Removing the purchase inside Cozy Pet deletes its local License Key record. Purchase records retained by Gumroad are governed by Gumroad's own retention requirements.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to kitestudio.dev@gmail.com.
