SUPPORT

Bring your cozy collection back.

Simple steps for restoring purchases, reinstalling, and getting help.

Restore the Complete Cozy Pack

  1. Open KiteStudio Cozy Pet from Chrome's side panel.
  2. Select Store.
  3. Copy the License Key from your Gumroad receipt.
  4. Paste the key into Already purchased? and select Unlock.
  5. If access does not update immediately, select Refresh licenses while connected to the internet.

Move or reinstall

Removing a Gumroad license from Cozy Pet deletes its local record from that installation. A valid License Key can be entered again after reinstalling the extension or clearing local data. Verification is configured not to increase the key's use count.

Purchase or refund help

Email kitestudio.dev@gmail.com with the Gumroad order number and a short description of the problem. Never send payment-card details and do not post the License Key publicly.

Privacy at a glance

Progress and to-dos remain in Chrome's local extension storage. Cozy Pet does not read webpage content or browsing history. Gumroad is contacted only when paid-content licensing needs activation or refresh. Read the complete Privacy Policy.
